## Problem

A funnel tells you where people leave. It doesn't tell you whether the people who stay are the ones you
wanted, or whether a drop-off is a design problem or an audience problem.

## Approach

Funnel and cohort analysis in SQL, exploratory work in Python, and an interactive Streamlit view so the
findings can be poked at rather than taken on faith. The output is a PRD and a set of recommendations,
because an insight that doesn't change a decision is just a chart.
